End-to-End Pipeline

Data Acquisition & Preprocessing

Standardized protocol from flight mission to normalized training tensors

1. UAV Flight Acquisition

DJI Matrice 300 / Phantom 4 RTK flying at 80–120m AGL with 20–45MP global shutter sensors, achieving 2.5–5.0 cm/pixel GSD.

80% OverlapRTK GNSSEPSG:4326

2. Georeferenced Tiling

Sliding window tiling into 256×256 px patches with 15% overlap (218 px stride) preserving raster affine transformations.

256×256 px15% OverlapMin 30% Valid

3. Albumentations Pipeline

On-the-fly random flips, rotation (±45°), color jitter (B/C/S=0.2), and random cropping (224×224) for robust scale invariance.

Flip & RotateColor JitterGaussian Blur

Trained & validated across UAVid 4K drone video, ODM Aukerman orthomosaics, ISPRS Potsdam/Vaihingen, and LandCover.ai.
